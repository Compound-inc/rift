# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teams that use AI chat every day for real work: drafting and editing, research, analysis of their own files, writing and reviewing code, and producing documents they hand to someone else. People work inside a shared workspace (an organization). Each member chats in their own threads and shares a project, a conversation, or a document with colleagues when that helps.

Workspace admins set the workspace up to standard: which models and tools are allowed, compliance and zero-data-retention rules, BYOK provider keys, members and roles, usage limits and billing. Some organizations self-host Rift. Individuals can also use Rift on their own, signed in or as a guest. Most use is on web and desktop. A mobile app is in development.

## Product Purpose

Rift is high-performance AI chat for teams: one fast workspace to chat with every major model, with the controls an organization needs. It exists to make AI chat feel instant and dependable. It keeps each provider's native behavior (reasoning, web search, code execution, citations). It keeps the team's context close at hand: projects, files, org knowledge, custom instructions and memory. And it gives admins policy, compliance and cost control without adding friction for everyone else.

A member's session succeeds when they leave thinking "that was fast, it understood what I needed, and I can trust and reuse what it produced." An admin succeeds when they trust that the workspace is configured to standard and stays that way.

## Brand Personality

Fast, precise, calm. The interface should deliver a dopamine hit of momentum: snappiness you can feel, a sense that Rift is actively helping you move faster. Users should leave feeling accomplished and satisfied, not drained. Tone is confident and quiet, never shouty.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise heaviness (Workday, SAP): dense, slow, ceremony-laden.
- Cluttered, hard-to-navigate layouts that bury the next action.
- Generic AI-chat skins (ChatGPT-clone gray bubble dumps).
- Feature sprawl on every turn: pickers, toggles and badges all asking for attention at once.
- AI slop: anything that screams "AI made that" template output.

## Design Principles

- Speed is a feeling. Every interaction should reward momentum: instant feedback, no perceptible waiting, motion that confirms progress rather than decorating it.
- Calm over clutter. Show the next decision, hide everything else. Allow density only where it earns trust (conversation history, sources, usage and admin tables), never as the default.
- Trust through clarity. People act on what Rift produces and share it with colleagues. Show where answers come from (sources, tool activity, the model that answered) and what state things are in (streaming, stopped, failed, summarized). Never leave either opaque.
- Structured, not stiff. Borrow Attio's record/table rigor and Notion's flexible low-friction surfaces, kept Linear-fast.
- Admin power, member calm. Policy and configuration live in settings for the people who own them. Members feel the effects without seeing the machinery.
- Earn every element. If a component, label, or accent doesn't help the user reach a good answer or a finished piece of work, it doesn't belong.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA baseline.
- Keyboard-first navigation (reinforces the snappy, Linear-style feel).
- Reduced-motion support honored via `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Color-blind-safe state communication (never color alone).
- Full English and Spanish localization; answers follow the user's language.
